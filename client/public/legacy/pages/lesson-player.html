Makharij al-Huruf — Articulation Points

Ustadha Fatima Zahra
Verified · 1,204 students

This lesson covers the seventeen articulation points (makharij) of Arabic letters — where each sound originates in the throat, tongue, or lips — and why precise makharij is the foundation of correct Tajweed before moving on to sifaat (letter characteristics).

In this lesson

  • The five major regions: al-jawf, al-halq, al-lisan, ash-shafatayn, al-khayshum
  • Letter-by-letter drilling with audio repeat for each makhraj
  • Common mispronunciations for Urdu and English native speakers
  • A short self-check quiz at the end of the lesson

Attachments

  • Makharij chart (PDF, downloadable for offline use)
  • Audio-only version (for low-bandwidth listening)
00:12Bismillah, welcome back. Today we begin the second half of our Tajweed foundations — the makharij, or exit points, of the Arabic letters.
01:04There are seventeen commonly recognised articulation points, grouped into five broader regions of the mouth and throat.
07:42Let's start with al-jawf, the empty space of the throat and mouth — this is where our three madd letters originate.
09:30Repeat after me slowly, and pay attention to where you feel the sound resonate.
