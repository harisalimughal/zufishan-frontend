What a review covers

Every engagement is scoped to your products and your risk areas. These are the building blocks we draw from.

  • Transaction testing

    We sample current and past transactions against the contracts and rulings that apply to them, and flag any that drifted from the approved structure.

    DeliverableExceptions register

  • Product and contract review

    New and existing products are read clause by clause, so the structure, pricing and paperwork all match what your Sharia board approved.

    DeliverableReview note for each product

  • Financial statement review

    We trace income, expenses and balance sheet items to find earnings that are not permissible and work out the amount to be purified.

    DeliverablePurification statement

  • Compliance reporting

    Findings are written for management and the board in plain language, ranked by seriousness, with the ruling or principle behind each one.

    DeliverablePeriodic compliance report

  • Aligning the Sharia function with the business

    We help your compliance function fit the way the business runs, so reviews inform planning instead of arriving after decisions are made.

    DeliverableGovernance and workflow notes

How an engagement runs

  1. 1

    Scoping call

    We agree what is in scope, the review period and the documents we will need.

  2. 2

    Fieldwork

    Our team reviews records, samples transactions and speaks with the people who run them.

  3. 3

    Reporting

    You receive a written report with findings, their seriousness and the reasoning behind each.

  4. 4

    Follow-up

    We check that corrective actions were completed and plan the next cycle with you.

Common questions

Short answers to what organisations usually ask first.

How often should a Sharia audit happen?

Most institutions review every quarter or half-year, with a full annual report. We suggest a cycle based on your transaction volume and product mix.

Is this only for financial institutions?

No. Any business that uses Islamic financing, holds investments or sells Sharia-sensitive products can ask for a review scoped to its own needs.

Will you need access to confidential records?

Only as far as the scope requires. We sign a confidentiality agreement before any records are shared and limit access to the review team.

Want a review scoped to your business?

Tell us what you offer and we will suggest a scope and a timeline.
