What we set up and review

Zakat has to be kept apart, assessed correctly and given to the right people. Each part below can be taken on its own or as a full programme.

  • Collection and fund separation

    Policies that keep Zakat apart from general donations and operating money, with clear rules for how each is recorded.

    DeliverableFund policy

  • Eligibility and disbursement rules

    A written process for confirming who qualifies as a recipient, how much is given and who approves it.

    DeliverableDisbursement procedure

  • Corporate Zakat assessment

    For businesses, we work out the Zakat base from your accounts and explain every line, including what is and is not included.

    DeliverableAssessment worksheet

  • Donor assurance reporting

    Statements that show donors how their Zakat was received and used, written so a non-specialist can follow them.

    DeliverableDonor report

  • Periodic audit of the mechanism

    We test the system against its own rules on a schedule and report any gap before it becomes a question of trust.

    DeliverableAudit findings

How an engagement runs

  2. 2

  4. 4

    Review

    We return on a schedule to test the system and update it as you grow.

Common questions

Short answers to what organisations usually ask first.

Can we use this for our own personal Zakat?

These services are built for organisations. For a personal estimate, use the Zakat Calculator, and put any specific question to a verified scholar through Fatwa Q&A.

Do we need a Sharia board of our own?

Not necessarily. We can act as your reference for rulings and review, or work alongside a board you already have.

Can donors receive the report?

Yes. We design the donor report so it can be shared as it is, and keep the supporting working papers on file for your records.
