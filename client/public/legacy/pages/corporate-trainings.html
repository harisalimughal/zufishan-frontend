Common questions

Short answers to what organisations usually ask first.

Can the content be adapted to our products?

Yes. Every programme is adjusted using your product documents and real scenarios, and your confidentiality is respected throughout.

What group size works best?

Around 10 to 30 people works well for discussion. Larger groups can be split into batches or run as a briefing.

Can sessions be delivered in Urdu?

Yes. Sessions can run in English, Urdu or a mix of both, depending on the audience.

Planning a session for your team?

Tell us who it is for and what they need to learn, and we will propose a programme.
